About us

Building the backbone of electric travel in India

EVGO brings together everything electric travel needs: clean vehicles that people want to ride, charging that is fast and reliable, places to rest while vehicles top up, and the hardware that makes it all work. We operate our own electric bus fleet and the charging network behind it, and we open that network to every EV driver, fleet and operator.

Because we run the buses, the stations, the lounges and the assembly line, we see the whole system. Our software ties it together, from charger health and tariffs to routes, seats and payments, so every station and every bus works harder.

Our vision

A country where every journey, city or intercity, two wheels or forty seats, can be made electric without a second thought.

Energy · Charging stations

Ultra-fast charging where India travels

EVGO builds and operates ultra-fast charging stations on highways and in cities, starting with the Guntur to Hyderabad corridor. Our hubs serve private cars, taxis, fleets and our own buses, and partner charge point operators can join the same network.

Highway corridors

Stations spaced so drivers and buses can travel intercity without range anxiety.

City and fleet hubs

Depot and hub charging for buses, taxis, delivery and logistics fleets.

Open to partners

Site hosts and charge point operators can list and manage their chargers on EVGO.

Captive solar generation Upcoming

Planned solar capacity to supply our stations with clean, lower-cost power of our own.

Energy · Our chargers

The right charger for every vehicle

From two-wheelers to trucks, every EVGO charger is connected, monitored and payable from one app.

DC

240 kW and 160 kW DC chargers

Ultra-fast CCS2 charging at our highway and city hubs, built to turn vehicles around in minutes.

  • 240 kW and 160 kW DC units
  • Cars, taxis, SUVs and light commercial vehicles
  • Multiple vehicles charged at once
Fleet

Bus and truck chargers

Heavy-duty depot and corridor chargers sized for electric buses and trucks.

  • Overnight depot and opportunity charging
  • Fleet-only access and scheduling
AC

2W and 3W chargers

Affordable charging points for scooters, motorcycles and auto-rickshaws.

  • Two- and three-wheeler connectors
  • Ideal for markets and residential sites

Smart and connected

Every charger runs on the open OCPP standard, so we monitor, update and control it remotely.

Driver app

iOS and Android app to find, book, start and pay for charging with UPI, alongside bus tickets.

Operator console

One console for chargers, sessions, tariffs and bus operations, for EVGO and partner operators.

Chargers built by EVGO
