About us

End to End electric travel for India

EVGO brings together everything electric travel needs: clean vehicles that people want to ride, charging that is fast and reliable, places to rest while vehicles top up, and the hardware that makes it all work. We operate our own electric bus fleet and the charging network behind it, and we open that network to every EV driver, fleet and operator.

Mobility · Our fleet

EVGO electric buses

Our electric intercity coaches offer quiet, comfortable, zero-emission rides. We started with Guntur to Hyderabad and are adding more routes. 70% of our bus charging already happens at EVGO's own stations, and we are working towards 100%, so we control uptime and cost End to End.

  • Air-conditioned electric coaches
  • Guntur to Hyderabad, with new routes being added
  • Seats booked online, with live trip information
  • 70% charged at EVGO's own stations, aiming for 100%

EVGO builds and operates ultra-fast charging stations on highways and in cities, starting with the Guntur to Hyderabad corridor and growing with every new route. Our hubs serve private cars, taxis, fleets and our own buses.

Manufacturing · Assembly line

AC chargers built by EVGO

We run our own assembly line for AC chargers up to 22 kW. Building in-house gives us control over quality, cost and lead times, and lets us scale our network faster.

  1. 01

    Components

    Power electronics, connectors and enclosures, checked before they reach the line.

  2. 02

    Assemble

    AC chargers up to 22 kW built on EVGO's own assembly line.

  3. 03

    Test

    Every unit tested for power, safety and network connectivity before it ships.

  4. 04

    Deploy

    Installed across EVGO's network, then monitored remotely from day one.
